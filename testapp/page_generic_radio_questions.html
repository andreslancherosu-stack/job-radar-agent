Fictional Company Careers - Screening Questions

Models a real, measured problem: multiple yes/no questions whose radio groups use generic, non-descriptive internal names (MultipleChoiceResponse0, MultipleChoiceResponse1, ...) and IDENTICAL "Yes"/"No" option labels across every question -- a page-wide label search or a positional/index-based guess cannot safely tell these apart. Used to test that a question is targeted by its own real text instead.
